02 / A little about me

Curious mind.
Thoughtful work.
Playful details.

I’m Toluwalope Akinbinu, a multidisciplinary designer based in Nigeria.

I work across product design, brand identity, graphic design and digital experiences. I enjoy taking an idea from the “what if?” stage to something people can understand, use and remember.

My approach balances structure with experimentation: understand the problem, find the clearest story, then make the details feel intentional. I’m continuing to grow through real projects, independent explorations and a daily commitment to making better work.

03 / How I think

My process, in three moves.

Good-looking is good. Good-looking with a reason is better.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the why.

Get clear on the people, the problem, and what success should look like before choosing a visual direction.

02 / EXPLORE

Make room for ideas.

Try different routes, challenge the obvious answer, and turn research and intuition into concepts worth testing.

03 / REFINE

Make every detail count.

Bring the strongest idea into a clear, consistent system and refine the experience until it feels intentional.
